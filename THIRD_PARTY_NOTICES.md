# Third-Party Audio Notices

Duck Desk includes edited and mastered derivatives of the following CC0 audio
assets. CC0 permits copying, modification, distribution, and commercial use
without attribution. Credits are included here to thank the creators and make
the audio provenance easy to audit.

## Kenney Audio Packs

Created and distributed by Kenney Vleugels
([Kenney.nl](https://kenney.nl/)). Each source pack is licensed under
[Creative Commons CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

- [UI Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-audio)
- [Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds)
- [Casino Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio)
- [Digital Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/digital-audio)
- [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds)
- [RPG Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio)

The original license text for each pack is stored beside the curated source
files under `scripts/audio-sources/kenney/`.

## UISFX Sound Recipes

Duck Desk uses lossless renders of deterministic sound recipes from
[UISFX](https://github.com/romainsimon/uisfx). The generated audio and its
recipes are licensed under
[Creative Commons CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

## Public-Domain Recordings

Selected tactile recordings come from
[sound-cc0](https://github.com/code4fukui/sound-cc0) and are dedicated to the
public domain under CC0 / the Unlicense.

The machine-readable `scripts/audio-sources/provenance.json` records every
source file, creator, license, source URL, acquisition date, upstream commit
where available, and SHA-256 hash. Curated source copies are included so all
90 mastered Duck Desk cues can be reproduced from a clean checkout.
