# Code of Conduct

Duck Desk uses a simple standard: be respectful, useful, and honest.

## Expected Behavior

- Help sellers and contributors feel welcome.
- Assume good intent, but be clear about risks and tradeoffs.
- Give actionable feedback.
- Respect privacy and do not ask for stream keys, account credentials, or private seller data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information.
- Pressuring maintainers or contributors for unpaid urgent support.
- Adding features that secretly collect data or bypass platform rules.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, block users, or reject contributions that violate this code.
